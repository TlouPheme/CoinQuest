# CoinQuest Presentation Script (5-7 Minutes)

## 1. Introduction (0:00 – 0:45)
**Screen: Dashboard**
"Good day. I am [Your Name], presenting **CoinQuest**, an Android budget tracker built with Kotlin and RoomDB. Our mission is to make financial management intuitive, visual, and rewarding."

## 2. Core Features (0:45 – 1:45)
**Action: Navigate to Add Transaction**
"The app features a secure Login and Registration system. Adding transactions is seamless—users can categorize spending and, most importantly, upload **Receipt Photos**. This ensures a digital paper trail for every cent spent."

## 3. Budget Dashboard (1:45 – 2:45)
**Action: Show Dashboard Color Changes**
"The Dashboard provides a real-time 'Financial Health Score.' It calculates your savings ratio and uses color-coding to warn you if you've exceeded your Maximum Expense limit or met your Minimum Income goal."

## 4. Spending Analytics (2:45 – 4:00)
**Action: Open Analytics and Change Date Range**
"Using MPAndroidChart, we visualize spending. The 'Red Limit Line' directly compares your actual spending against your budget goals. Our 'Smart Spending Insights' at the bottom automatically identify your highest expense categories."

## 5. Gamification & Custom Features (4:00 – 5:15)
**Action: Open Rewards/Achievements**
"To keep users engaged, we have a Leveling and Badge system. Users earn XP for consistency. We've also added a **Savings Goal Tracker**, allowing users to set and visualize progress toward long-term targets like an Emergency Fund."

## 6. Architecture & Testing (5:15 – 6:00)
**Action: Show GitHub Actions/Test Folder in IDE**
"Technically, the app uses MVVM architecture with Coroutines and Flow. We've implemented JUnit tests for business logic and a GitHub Actions pipeline for automated CI/CD builds and testing."

## 7. Conclusion (6:00 – End)
"CoinQuest turns budgeting into a quest for financial freedom. Thank you for your time."
